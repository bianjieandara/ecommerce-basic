## What your customers see

**A tidy catalogue.** Products sorted into categories, with their versions (size, colour…), sale prices, filters, and a search box that suggests products as the person types. When someone views a product, similar products appear too.

**Checkout on one page.** The customer chooses how to receive the order, can add invoice details, and pays the way they prefer. There are **five payment methods**:

- Card, through the dLocal Go payment processor.
- PayPal.
- WhatsApp: the order is completed by chat.
- Bank transfer<RegionText intl="." mx=", to your CLABE account number." ec=", to your bank account." />
- Cash when the order is delivered.

**Order tracking.** Each customer gets a private link to follow their purchase, and can download their invoice as a PDF.

**English and Spanish.** The whole store is in both languages, and each has its own web address.

**Made for phones.** Most purchases happen on a phone, so the store was designed for small screens first: large buttons that are easy to tap, and no animations for people who have them turned off.

**Ready for Google.** Every page has its own title and description, so Google and other search engines can find your products. When someone shares a link, it appears with a picture and a title.

**Fast.** Pages are saved so they open faster, and pictures are sized to fit the screen. In a Google Lighthouse test (a free tool that measures how fast and how well a page is built), run as if from a phone, the store scored between 96 and 99 for performance, 100 for best practices and SEO, and between 96 and 100 for accessibility.

## What you manage yourself

Everything is handled from the panel, without coding:

- **Products:** versions, categories, features and a picture library.
- **Inventory:** stock levels, with a record of every change.
- **Orders:** the status and history of each one. From there you can reply to the customer on WhatsApp and, for card or PayPal payments, refund the money.
- **Invoices:** view them, send them again, download them as PDFs, or generate them again.
- **Pages:** a visual editor for the home page and the content pages.
- **Emails, shipping, testimonials and reports:** the wording of the emails, shipping methods, customer reviews, and sales reports.
- **Settings:** colours, fonts and the rest of the store.
- **Users:** there are two roles. The **Owner** can do everything, including settings and user accounts. The **Editor** handles day-to-day work, and does not see settings, user accounts or the refund button.

See the [admin guide](/en/admin/overview/) for every screen.

## What happens without you having to ask

- **Automatic notices.** Your customer gets an email at each step of their order. If you turn it on, they also get **WhatsApp messages**, using wording Meta has already approved. It is an extra with its own cost.
- **Held stock.** When someone pays by card, the product is reserved while the payment is confirmed. If the payment is not completed in time, the product becomes available again. You never sell more units than you have.
- **Retries.** If sending an email or creating an invoice fails, the store tries again. If it keeps failing, it flags the task for an owner to review.
- **Basic security.** Protection against robots on forms, a limit on repeated login attempts, checks on payment notifications so nobody can fake them, private links, and browser protections. More detail in [Running the store](/en/clients/operations/).

## Before you decide

### How much does it cost?

There are three kinds of cost:

- **The platform:** about 5 USD a month per Cloudflare account, where the store runs. You can choose the monthly or yearly maintenance plan.
- **Your domain:** the yearly price from your domain registrar.
- **Fees from each provider:** dLocal Go and PayPal charge fees on each sale, and Meta charges for each WhatsApp message sent, depending on its category and country. The store adds no fee of its own. Email has no such cost.

Separately, if you would rather have someone else make the changes to your store, that is optional and has fixed prices: see [Changes we make for you](#changes-we-make-for-you).

This guide does not include the price of building and launching your store. Your contact will quote it.

### How long does it take, and what do you provide?

The timeline depends on how quickly you deliver what is asked for and on approvals (WhatsApp templates are often approved within minutes or a day). This guide does not set a total timeline; your contact will confirm it.

You provide:

- A domain name, or a subdomain of your current website.
- Your business details for invoices: legal name, <RegionText intl="tax ID" mx="RFC and tax regime" ec="RUC number" />, address and tax rate.
- Your products, with prices, stock levels and square photos at least 1000 px.
- **dLocal Go** (card) and **PayPal Business** accounts. They start in test mode.
- A sending email address on a domain you own, so Gmail and Outlook trust order emails.
- Legal texts (privacy, terms, shipping and returns). The store includes templates, but someone should review them for your country and business.
- Only if you want them: Meta Business and WhatsApp Business accounts, and a Meta Pixel.

Most of the time is spent by you filling the store: products, pages, shipping and email wording.

### Who owns my customers and data? What if I leave?

- Your store has its own database, images, task queue and analytics, separate from other stores. Your orders, customers, products and photos live in your store.
- If the store runs on your own Cloudflare account, you keep full ownership. If it runs on the agency's account, the data lives in that account.
- Because each store is independent, it can be moved to another account.
- This guide does not say exactly what happens if you end the service: timing, handing over data, or the cost of leaving. Ask for it in writing before you sign.

### Can I change things myself?

Yes, most things, from the panel and without code: name, logo, colours and fonts; the home page and content pages; email wording; shipping options and prices; payment methods and bank details; taxes and invoicing; and who can sign in to the panel.

Some things do need a developer: a section or style that only you will have, or connecting a different payment or invoicing provider. Custom sections are not loaded by the store yet, so ask your developer to confirm their status before you are promised one.

If you can do it yourself but would rather have someone else do it, you can also ask the agency or your developer to make the changes for you. The prices are in [Changes we make for you](#changes-we-make-for-you).

## Changes we make for you

You can change almost everything yourself from the panel, but many people prefer to have someone else do it: the text of a page, a new product, the WhatsApp number, the bank account. It is an option, not a requirement. Prices depend on your country (use the selector above) and **already include VAT**: what you see is what you pay, and you get an invoice.

### What counts as a change

Everything is measured in **credits**. One credit is one small change.

| Size                | Credits | Examples                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Small**           | 1       | Change a piece of text, the WhatsApp number or the bank account; change the price or stock of up to 5 products; show or hide a product; swap a picture.                  |
| **Medium**          | 2       | Add up to 3 products with their pictures; set up a promotion or a banner; adjust shipping and its prices; edit a whole page or the wording of the emails.                 |
| **Large**           | 5       | Create a new page; load a catalogue of up to 50 products; rearrange the home page.                                                                                        |
| **Bigger than that** | Quote  | You are told the price before we start.                                                                                                                                   |

### How credits and plans work

A **credit** is the unit work is measured in. Each change you ask for costs 1, 2 or 5 credits depending on its size (see the table above). There are three ways to get credits, and you can combine them:

- **Pay per change.** There are no saved credits: you pay the price of the change and we do it.
- **Pack.** You buy a number of credits at once. They are used up as you ask for changes and last 12 months from the purchase. Each credit costs less than paying per change.
- **Monthly or yearly plan.** You receive credits on a regular basis and a faster response. On the monthly plan, each month's credits are used that month and do not carry over. On the yearly plan, the credits for all 12 months are one pool: you use them whenever you like while the plan is active.

**How they are used up.** Before we start, we tell you how many credits what you asked for costs, and we wait for your go-ahead. If a request has several changes, they add up. If something turns out to be bigger than it looked, we tell you before going on; credits are never taken without you knowing.

**What happens when they run out.** You can buy a pack, pay for a single change, or move up a plan. While you have a plan, your requests keep the response time of your plan even if you pay for one change separately.

**An example.** You are in Mexico and have the Grow plan (10 credits a month). This month you ask to change the WhatsApp number (1 credit), add 3 products with pictures (2 credits) and set up a promotion banner (2 credits). You used 5 of your 10 credits. The other 5 are lost when the month ends. With the Yearly Grow plan, those 5 credits would still be there for a busier season.

**When they start and end.** Credits and plans start the day your payment is confirmed. The monthly plan renews every month as long as you pay for it, and you can cancel it at any time for the following month. The yearly plan lasts 12 months.

### Yearly plans

You pay once and get 12 months of service, with **2 months free**. The year's credits are one pool: you use them when you need them, and they do not run out at the end of each month. This suits you best if your store has busy seasons, such as Christmas or Mother's Day.

A **store check-up** is a full pass we do ourselves: we review texts, prices, stock and links, test that buying works from start to finish, and send you a short summary of what we found. It does not use credits.

### Which one suits you

- **A change now and then:** pay per change.
- **Several changes a year, with no fixed date:** a credit pack.
- **Changes every week** (promotions, new products, prices): a monthly plan.
- **You know you will keep going and have busy seasons:** a yearly plan, which costs less and lets you use the credits when you need them.

### How to ask and how to pay

- You write by WhatsApp or email what you want changed and, if needed, send the pictures or the text. We confirm how many credits it costs before we start.
- You pay by <RegionText intl="PayPal or card." mx="SPEI bank transfer or card." ec="bank transfer or deposit." /> Monthly plans are charged at the start of each month, and yearly plans once at the start of the year.
- Without a plan, changes are done in about 5 business days. With a plan, within the response time in the table.

### What is not included

The domain, the platform where the store runs and the payment providers' fees are paid separately (see [How much does it cost?](#how-much-does-it-cost)). Managing your ads is not included either, and work that needs custom code is quoted separately.
