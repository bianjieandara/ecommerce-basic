## What you manage yourself

All from the panel, without programming: products and stock, orders and invoices, the pages through a visual editor, the text of
the emails, shipping and settings. There are two roles: **owner**, who can do everything, and **editor**, who handles the day to
day without seeing the settings or the refund button.

If you want to see every screen before deciding, they are all in the [panel guide](/en/admin/overview/).

### Who owns my customers and data? What if I leave?

- Your store has its own database, images, task queue and analytics, separate from other stores. Your orders, customers, products and photos live in your store.
- If the store runs on your own Cloudflare account, you keep full ownership. If it runs on the agency's account, the data lives in that account.
- Because each store is independent, it can be moved to another account.
- If you ever decide to leave, get the exit terms in writing before you sign: timing, handing over your data, and the cost.

### Can I change things myself?

Yes, most things, from the panel and without code: name, logo, colours and fonts; the home page and content pages; email wording; shipping options and prices; payment methods and bank details; taxes and invoicing; and who can sign in to the panel.

Some things do need a developer: a section or style that only you will have, or connecting a different payment or invoicing provider. Custom sections are not loaded by the store yet, so ask your developer to confirm their status before you are promised one.

If you can do it yourself but would rather have someone else do it, you can also ask the agency or your developer to make the changes for you. The prices are in [Changes we make for you](/en/clients/changes-service/).

## What if I would rather you did it?

You can change almost everything yourself from the panel, but plenty of people would rather someone else did it: the text on a page, a new product, the WhatsApp number. It is an option, not an obligation, and it has fixed prices with tax included.

See [Changes we make for you](/en/clients/changes-service/).
