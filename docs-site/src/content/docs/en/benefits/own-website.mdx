**Your own website** is an internet address that belongs to you (`yourbusiness.com`), with your words, your photos, your
prices and your phone number. It is not a social media profile or a WhatsApp number: it is where the person who is already
looking for you lands, and where they decide whether to buy from you.

:::note
The figures in this section are Mexican and come from 2025–2026. Each one names its source; every source, with its date, is
in [Mexico by the numbers](/en/benefits/mexico-data/).
:::

## Why Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp are not enough

Social media works, and works well, for being **discovered**: 99 million social media identities in Mexico, 74.9% of the
population (Digital 2026). The trouble is what happens after the discovery.

The healthy answer is both: social media and WhatsApp for the conversation, your website to inform and to charge.

## What your own website will not do on its own

:::caution
A website does not create visits by itself. People have to know it exists: your Google business profile, your social
accounts, your WhatsApp, your cards, your shop. And it needs your content: decent photos, current prices and text that tells
the truth. This guide says so plainly because that is the part that will cost you the most work, and no technology fixes it.
:::
