**Your own online store** is your catalog with a cart and a checkout: people buy on their own, at any hour, without you
answering a message to close the sale. It is not a stall in a marketplace or a WhatsApp catalog: it is yours, and it pays
nobody a commission for selling.

:::note
The figures in this section are Mexican and come from 2025–2026. Each one names its source; every source, with its date, is
in [Mexico by the numbers](/en/benefits/mexico-data/).
:::

## Your store against a marketplace

A marketplace brings you visits; that is real and worth saying. What it charges for bringing them is just as real, and it is
charged on every sale, forever. These are the costs each platform publishes:

<FeatureGrid layout="card" items={[
  {"icon": "cart", "title": "Mercado Libre México", "text": "A commission of 8%–16% on a Clásica listing and 12.5%–20.5% on Premium (the one that allows interest-free monthly instalments). On top of that, a fixed charge of $25, $30 or $37 per unit on products under $299. No monthly cost: the platform brings the visits, and the customer is theirs."},
  {"icon": "box", "title": "Amazon México", "text": "A referral fee from 8%, with most categories between 10% and 15% and a minimum fee per item. The Professional plan subscription is added on top. It also brings the visits, and the customer is theirs."},
  {"icon": "star", "title": "Your own store", "text": "0% platform commission and no per-unit charge. You pay about 5 USD a month whether you sell 1 or 1,000, your domain, and the fee of whoever processes the payment (PayPal publishes 3.95% + a fixed fee domestically). You bring the visits, and the customer is yours."},
]} />

**A 1,000-peso order, in numbers.** On Mercado Libre, depending on the category and the listing type, between **$80 and
$205** stays behind as commission. On Amazon, between **$80 and $150**. In your store the platform keeps **$0** and you only
pay whoever processes the payment (with PayPal's published domestic rate, about $40 plus its fixed fee). The difference does
not show on the first order: it shows on order number two hundred.

:::tip
It is not «one or the other». Plenty of people sell on both: the marketplace so people discover them, their own store so the
customer who already knows them comes back without paying a commission. The expensive part is having someone else's shop
window as your only one.
:::

## What stops people from finishing a purchase

This is what AMVO measures in its Estudio de Venta Online 2026, and what a well-built store answers:

| What holds the shopper back                                             | What the store does                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A high shipping cost is the main reason for abandoning                  | You set the shipping methods and prices, and they are shown before paying            |
| 75% of interruptions come from missing information or having to ask     | A product page with variants, photos, features and your own text; search and filters |
| 71% come from problems with the buying and payment process              | Checkout on a single page, five payment methods and fast pages                       |
| 70% expect delivery within a week at most; 32% the same or the next day | Emails and order tracking through a private link, so nobody has to ask               |
| 90% want home delivery; 21% prefer to collect                           | Home delivery and store pickup, each with its own price                              |
| 8 in 10 feel safe buying online, but they ask for secure payment        | Payments through recognised providers and bot protection on the forms                |

## What a store does not solve

:::caution
The store charges; packing and shipping are still your job. And delivery is where people get angriest: among those who had a
problem, 45% received the product damaged, 38% late and 33% incomplete (AMVO 2026). Returns are real too: 22% of shoppers
returned something, and in fashion it is 28%.

Two more things, so they do not surprise you: visits do not arrive on their own (your Google profile, your social accounts
and your WhatsApp move those), and interest-free monthly instalments depend on your payment provider, so ask whether it
offers them and at what cost before promising any.
:::
